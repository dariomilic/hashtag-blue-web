## 2. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device when you visit it. They enable the website to function correctly and remember visitors' basic settings and choices. In addition to cookies, we use similar technologies (for example, browser local storage), to which this Policy applies as appropriate.

## 3. Cookies we use

This website uses only essential first-party cookies required for basic operation and for remembering visitors' basic settings. Essential cookies are placed without consent on the basis of Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (the Company's legitimate interest in ensuring the proper and secure operation of the website) and the exemption under Article 224(3) of the Electronic Communications Act.

The Company does not use third-party analytics, functional or marketing cookies on this website (including Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, Hotjar or similar tools), nor does it embed third-party scripts that would place cookies on your device. If we introduce such tools in the future, this Policy will be updated and we will request your prior consent before they are placed, in accordance with Article 6(1)(a) GDPR.

This website uses only essential first-party cookies to remember visitors' basic settings and maintain a secure session. You can view the exact names and duration of cookies in your browser settings.
